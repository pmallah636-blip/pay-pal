# Delivery notes (DEMO)

- Three static pages are included: Home, Services, and Contact.
- Shared navigation links the pages together.
- The stylesheet includes a responsive breakpoint at 700px.
- The contact form includes name, email, and message fields with browser validation.
- Client-side JavaScript displays an in-page confirmation after valid submission.
- No backend or email service is connected; the browser confirmation is a local demonstration only.
- `README.md` explains how to open the website locally.
